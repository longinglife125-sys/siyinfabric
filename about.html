About SIYIN

Dongguan Siyin Sports Goods Co., Ltd. is a performance fabric manufacturer specializing in neoprene materials and stretch fabrics. With over 20 years of manufacturing experience since 2002, our team has developed expertise in fabric production, lamination technology and customized material solutions. We support customers from fabric selection and development to production and quality control, supplying materials for sportswear, outdoor products, bags and functional applications.
